## Project Overview

This project explores the creation of realistic fluid simulations for visual effects in film and commercial work. Using Houdini's FLIP solver, I developed a pipeline for creating believable water interactions.

## Technical Details

- **Software**: Houdini, Nuke
- **Render Engine**: Mantra / Redshift
- **Duration**: 3 weeks

## My Role

Lead FX Artist responsible for simulation setup, shader development, and final compositing.
