## Project Overview – *Joy-Con Stick Integration*

Created a CG Joy-Con analog stick and placed it next to a real controller, matching scale, materials, and lighting for a seamless integration.

## Responsibilities

- Modeled and rendered the Joy-Con stick in Maya.
- Matched the CG asset with the real controller in scale and lighting.
- Composited the render with live-action footage in Nuke.

## Technical Details

- Modeling and rendering: Maya  
- Compositing: Nuke
