## Project Overview – *The Diplomat*

Contributed to the ocean sequence and large-scale explosion effects, combining water interaction, destruction, and environmental dynamics to support the narrative intensity of the scene.

## Responsibilities

- Developed the ocean environment and integrated explosion effects.
- Created dynamic water interaction to enhance realism during the blast sequence.
- Supported the lighting team by optimizing the modeling–texturing–lighting workflow.
- Improved pipeline efficiency and visual consistency across multiple shots.

## Technical Details

- Built and refined ocean simulations to interact naturally with explosion forces.
- Integrated FX elements into the lighting pipeline for smoother handoff between departments.
- Enhanced workflow processes to streamline asset preparation and look development.
